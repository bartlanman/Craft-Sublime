# Employee Handbook Project

**Basic City Beer Co. / The Foundry Campus**  
**Status:** Working / discovery and constitutional drafting  
**Started:** November 6, 2026

## Purpose

Create an employee handbook shaped by the lived cultural experience of **Craft Sublime**. Rather than attach a philosophical introduction to ordinary policies, this project will translate Craft Sublime into learnable, observable, and sustainable practices across Basic City's departments.

**Working design question:** How can Basic City cultivate the conditions in which people do excellent work, receive and pass along value, develop capacity, and strengthen the place and one another?

## Foundational relationship (decision, November 6, 2026)

**A: Craft Sublime is the philosophical foundation. B: Basic City is one particular expression of it.** The handbook translates the universal concepts into the concrete experience of employment at Basic City; it must not redefine the entire philosophy as site-specific to the brewery.

The TOS repository preserves earlier formulations that describe Craft Sublime as specifically carved for Basic City. Treat those as part of the conceptual history, not as an unexamined constraint on the current project. See [TOS source review](TOS-SOURCE-REVIEW.md).

## Intended handbook system

1. **The Living Culture:** shared purpose, ways of working, leadership expectations, learning and stewardship, and recurring practices.
2. **Employment Framework:** authoritative and legally reviewed employment policies, pay, leave, reporting, conduct and safety.
3. **House Guides:** role- and space-specific training and operating procedures for Taproom, Brewery, Foundry, Fitchen, Showroom, Sip City, and cross-campus functions.

The system should read as a coherent employee experience, while maintaining clear distinctions among values, policies, and procedures.

## Source roles and governing trajectory

**The new handbook is not a revision of the old handbook.** The conceptual trajectory flows from the evolving Craft Sublime philosophy toward an employee experience. Earlier crew handbooks do not determine the new structure, developmental model, or meaning of Craft Sublime.

1. **Evolving Craft Sublime work:** original foundation documents and subsequent conceptual developments are primary material for constructing the cultural philosophy. Later formulations are not automatically correct or final; trace changes and evaluate tensions.
2. **Dissertation as a map, not a final canon:** *Craft Sublime: A Dissertation* (November 5, 2026) supplies synthesis, lineage, contradictions, and suggested tests. Return to the underlying sources when the constitution raises a question.
3. **Related inquiry and critique:** TOS, TOS Shadow, Meaning-Field, and other relevant projects may illuminate, challenge, or extend the cultural understanding. Do not automatically merge different systems.
4. **Former employee handbooks and floor guides:** historical/operational references only. Mine them for substance, illustrative Basic City context, actual practices, and lessons learned. **Do not inherit their chapter structure, vocabulary, stage assumptions, or policy choices by default.**
5. **Present Basic City conditions:** validate any operational examples or commitments against current reality and responsible managers. Employment policies require separate legal and organizational review.

Where sources differ, preserve chronology and articulate what changed. Distinguish **source-derived doctrine**, **new interpretive proposals**, **historical practices**, and **verified current operating facts**. Avoid treating a synthesis as proof of its own claims.

## Historical-handbook extraction protocol

For each old handbook item, record: (a) what it says; (b) whether it reflects a past or current operation; (c) useful example, process, or cultural insight; (d) any philosophical assumption that may now be superseded; and (e) whether to **adapt**, **verify**, **archive**, or **exclude**. Never silently import an old provision into the Cultural Constitution.

## Working rules

- **Shape before vocabulary:** employees should be able to practice the culture without mastering its philosophical lexicon.
- **Give every idea a body:** connect principles to something people can observe, learn or do.
- **The story cannot outrun the operation:** do not publish nonexistent benefits, safety systems, or management practices as current facts.
- **Culture is reciprocal:** clarify what the organization owes employees, not only what employees owe the company.
- **Stewardship is a practice, not a human ranking:** keep development, job competence and assigned authority separate.
- **Invitation, not belief requirement:** metaphysical language can illuminate the origin story but is not a condition of employment.
- **Correction is part of culture:** disagreement and reporting unsafe or improper practices must remain possible without retaliation.
- **Prefer small, tested rituals over multiplying abstractions.**

## Constitution change control — explicit owner sign-off required

**Cultural Constitution Draft 0.1 is a protected baseline.** The founder has affirmed that its present wording, rhythm, and coherence "ring" and must be preserved.

- Do **not** change `CULTURAL-CONSTITUTION.md` without Bart's explicit approval of the proposed wording.
- New foundation/vault research may enrich our interpretation but does not automatically authorize edits.
- Record potential changes separately as **proposals** with source evidence, rationale, and what would be gained or lost.
- No approval can be inferred from a request to review, compare, summarize, or import sources.
- If approved, make only the specifically approved changes and record them in the project history.

## Version discipline
