# TOS Source Review — Craft Sublime and the Employee Handbook

**Reviewed:** November 6, 2026  
**Repository:** [bartlanman/TOS](https://github.com/bartlanman/TOS)  
**Status:** Source review, not an amendment to the Cultural Constitution

## Governing project decision

**Craft Sublime is the wider philosophical foundation; Basic City is a particular expression.** This is the current handbook-project framing. The TOS archive includes earlier, more site-bound descriptions. Those are valuable evidence of how the concept developed, not a mandate to retain their older limits.

## Findings that matter for the constitution

### 1. Culture and mythology

The reformed consolidation distinguishes **culture as tending** and **mythology as telling**. Culture has four faces — cultivate, dwell, care, worship — and two scales, the inner cultivation of a participant and the shared cultivation of language, spaces, tools, and rhythms. A living mythology remains responsive to reception rather than hardening into repetition.

**Handbook implication:** express the culture as lived practices, with the story of Basic City as a meaningful carrier rather than a substitute for experience.

### 2. Maker, made thing, receiver

The reformed consolidation replaces the two-register interface with a **three-pole synergy**: maker, made thing and receiver. The maker tends rather than originates; the made thing carries; the receiver participates. The resulting encounter cannot be guaranteed or forced.

**Handbook implication:** do not write a service doctrine in which staff alone are responsible for manufacturing someone else's emotion or transformation.

### 3. No automatic inheritance of old graphics

The older cycle and v2.1 patches expressly say they are **deprecated**. They are usable as historical material and as an inventory of concrete campus work (pre-shift alignment, bar, brewing, stage, infrastructure, handoff), but their dual-architecture mechanism is not current authority.

### 4. The older succession is narrower than our present decision

The Succession describes Craft Sublime as TOS carved down to 'everything-that-isn't-Basic-City' removed. The reformed consolidation describes a culture of maker/made thing/receiver in more general terms, while still pointing to Basic City as an expression.

**Do not erase this distinction:** it documents a conceptual evolution or unresolved framing. The handbook project will treat Craft Sublime as philosophically general while documenting the site-specific instantiation in Basic City. Later primary texts may sharpen this.

### 5. Tether / guardrail

The Succession translates the guardrail across TOS, Craft Sublime, marketing and social expression: an account of the thing must remain accountable to the thing itself.

**Handbook implication:** match cultural claims to actual conditions and practices. Do not use narrative to mask missing equipment, resources, safety measures, or other operative facts.

## What this review does not establish

- It does not establish the entire **current** Craft Sublime canon. TOS points out of its own corpus to a standalone /Craft Sublime/ vault.
- It does not establish which earlier operational practices are still in force.
- It does not establish any new employment policy.
- It does not prove the philosophical universality of Craft Sublime; it records the project's chosen distinction and the source lineage.
