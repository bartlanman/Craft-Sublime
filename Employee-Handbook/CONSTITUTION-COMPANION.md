# Constitution Companion — Source Reading and Interpretive Map

**Project:** Basic City Employee Handbook  
**Version:** 0.1 — Foundation review, November 6, 2026  
**Status:** Research and interpretation only; **not** an amendment  
**Protected text:** [CULTURAL-CONSTITUTION.md](CULTURAL-CONSTITUTION.md), Draft 0.1 — no change without Bart's explicit sign-off  
**Source corpus:** User-supplied `Craft Sublime.zip` (Obsidian vault, 507 ZIP entries including macOS metadata); the full source texts were read from their Markdown paths within `Craft Sublime/`.

## Purpose

The Cultural Constitution already has a strong voice and coherent arc. This companion asks **why it works**, what the reformed sources actually support, and where its employee-facing commitments are **interpretations** rather than quotations or already adopted Basic City practices. It does not treat added vocabulary as an improvement in itself.

The governing relationship is: **Craft Sublime as a broader philosophical foundation; Basic City as a particular practical expression.** The constitution addresses the latter without reducing the former to Basic City.

## How to read the evidence

- **Source-derived:** stated in a named original document.
- **Interpretation:** our translation of that philosophy into the constitution's language or employment context.
- **Proposed commitment:** a principle the organization might adopt; not proof that it is already official policy.
- **Source status matters:** the `00_Manifesto.md` calls itself a *working consolidation*; `04_Grammar_of_the_Relay.md` calls itself *canonical* and a *constitutional document for the framework*. The latter is a philosophical constitution, not an HR constitution.
- **Chronology matters:** `02_Culture/00_Culture_Overview.md` labels itself a *legacy pre-reformation seed*, and some Foundation files are working companions. Do not flatten their status labels.
- The vault contains metaphysical and etymological claims. This review reports them as **claims of the source**, not as independent historical or empirical verification.

## Section-by-section reading

### I. Our Beginning — A Culture Is Cultivated

**Primary sources**
- `00_Foundation/00_Manifesto.md`: culture as sustained cultivation of conditions in which tuned makers, made things, and receivers meet; culture as tending, mythology as telling; document status *working consolidation*.
- `00_Foundation/02_Chain_of_Transmission.md`: field → maker → made thing → receiver → sublime → transcendence → field re-perceived. Crucially, it states that the encounter invites rather than compels.
- `00_Foundation/04_Grammar_of_the_Relay.md`: field, signal, capacity, transparency; relay and trace; *canonical lexicon*.
- `01_Praxis/00_Praxis_Overview.md`: three poles in synergy; marked *legacy pillar*, useful for lineage but not sole authority.

**What the constitution gets right**
It defines Craft Sublime through **what is cultivated** rather than promising that an extraordinary feeling can be produced on demand. It names beer, concerts, meals, and service without making the company the origin of the philosophy. This is faithful to the source's distinction between the maker's work and the receiver's participation.

**Distinction worth holding**
The source says the maker is a relay, not the ultimate origin. The current constitution says people make and contribute, which is appropriate employee language; the deeper source discourages proprietary claims over what emerges. The philosophical language of field and transcendence need not appear in the constitution for that structure to survive.

**No amendment proposed.**

### II. Culture — The Work of Tending

**Primary sources**
- `00_Foundation/00_Manifesto.md`: four faces of *colere* (cultivate, dwell, care, worship) and inner/outer culture.
- `02_Culture/00_Culture_Overview.md`: two scales, *cultura animi* and *cultura communis*; culture as sustained tending; marked legacy seed and points to later live sources.
- `00_Foundation/04_Grammar_of_the_Relay.md`: “Culture: What remains after repeated care.”
- `00_Foundation/28_Regeneration.md`: continuing return and renewal.

**What the constitution gets right**
The current text—“It is what we repeatedly cultivate”—compresses a substantial theory into a sentence that can govern a workplace without demanding adherence to the source's spiritual language. Its examples (preparation, spaces, work, welcome, correction) put culture into repeatable acts.

**Interpretive step**
“The company has a responsibility to provide the conditions for good work” and reciprocal employee responsibility are **proposed employer–employee commitments**, not quotations from the philosophical source. This is a substantive, appropriate ethical development, but it requires organizational adoption and must not be confused with an existing HR policy.

**No amendment proposed.**

### III. The Two Hands — Building and Clearing

**Primary sources**
- `00_Foundation/03_The_Two_Hands_and_the_Transparent_Relay.md`: status *working consolidation*; transmission as transparency; signal, capacity, identity/interference and a hidden third hand.
- `00_Foundation/04_Grammar_of_the_Relay.md`: śruti receives/clears, poiesis builds; signal, capacity, transparency as the three variables.
- `00_Foundation/01a_The_Plain_Glossary.md`: plain-language presentation calls the two hands receiving and making, and *additive/subtractive* the floor-facing “add and clear.”

**What the constitution gets right**
“Build what is needed” and “clear what obstructs” are a compelling floor translation. It incorporates skill development and ego-clearing without turning the philosophy into a technical lesson.

**Precision note**
**Building/clearing are practical motions, not a complete definition of the deeper model**. The source also distinguishes reception (śruti), making (poiesis), and identity/transparency. Do not revert to the older, superseded equation “Craft = additive, Sublime = subtractive.” That would misstate the reformed framework. The constitution currently avoids that error.

**No amendment proposed.**

### IV. The Relay — What Passes Through Us

**Primary sources**
- `00_Foundation/04_Grammar_of_the_Relay.md`: maker attends, chooses, forms; thing stores and carries; receiver interprets and continues; trace remains and becomes part of the field.
- `00_Foundation/02_Chain_of_Transmission.md`: the maker is a relay, made thing is fruit and vessel, receiver completes the chain.
- `00_Foundation/05_Relay_and_Resonance.md`: expanded relay and resonance treatment.

**What the constitution gets right**
The brewer → bartender → guest example makes the abstract movement visible as interdependent work. The emphasis on preparing the next person to succeed captures the source's opposition to ownership-as-obstruction.

**Boundary**
This does not imply that every job must be interchangeable or that professional accountability vanishes. Skills, role duties, and authority remain separately defined in the employee handbook.

**No amendment proposed.**

### V. The Encounter — Making and Receiving

**Primary sources**
- `00_Foundation/02_Chain_of_Transmission.md`: the maker cannot guarantee the receiver's crossing; the encounter is invitation, not coercion.
- `01_Praxis/00_Praxis_Overview.md`: maker, made thing, receiver each participate through distinct work.
- `00_Foundation/04_Grammar_of_the_Relay.md`: alignment is resonance, not merely agreement.

**What the constitution gets right**
It acknowledges the receiver's independence and refuses to make hospitality into emotional engineering. “We do not presume to determine what another person must experience or feel” is an effective ethical translation.

**Interpretive step**
Applying the same stance to coworkers is an employee-culture extension. It should remain a reciprocal practice, not a reason to override explicit service duties, legitimate feedback, or guest safety.

**No amendment proposed.**

### VI. Becoming — The Development of Capacity

**Primary sources**
- `00_Foundation/15_The_Identity_Progression.md`: *working* account of Visitor, Initiate, Regular, Insider, Steward; stages are positions in an arc of formation, not employee job grades.
- `00_Foundation/15a_The_Becoming_The_Betweens_of_Formation.md`: *working doctrine*; becoming is in the verbs between stations (participation, fluency, trust, relinquishment, renewal), not in possession of a label.
- `00_Foundation/04_Grammar_of_the_Relay.md`: steward's capacity exceeds personal need; work passes without possession.

**What the constitution gets right**
It takes stewardship as an act of helping others become capable. The phrase that stewardship is not a permanent label of superior worth protects the central anti-ownership insight from becoming a workplace caste system.

**Important boundary**
Developmental metaphors **must not** be used as subjective performance scoring, promotion guarantees, pay bands, or a judgment of a person's spiritual maturity. Those require separately defined, objectively reviewable workplace criteria.

**No amendment proposed.**

### VII. Integrity — The Work Must Be Real

**Primary sources**
- `00_Foundation/04_Grammar_of_the_Relay.md`: transparency means less distortion from ownership.
- `00_Foundation/23_The_Turn_and_the_Ground.md`: turn interpretation back to the real object/ground.
- `00_Foundation/24_The_Puffer_and_the_Adept.md`: guard against theatrical inflation.
- `00_Foundation/22_The_Field_Kit.md`: “give every idea a body” through a tool, trigger, number, or script.
- The dissertation's critical assessment separately warns that story can outrun operation.

**What the constitution gets right**
It puts accuracy, acknowledgment of error, and criticism ahead of appearances. That is a faithful operational implication of the tether and transparency.

**Proposed commitment**
The employee-facing commitments to report safety problems and misconduct and to hold leadership accountable are **important new employment principles**, not mere paraphrases of the philosophy. They need consistent reporting channels and formal protections in a legally reviewed employment framework.

**No amendment proposed.**

### VIII. Regeneration — What We Leave Behind

**Primary sources**
- `00_Foundation/28_Regeneration.md`: marked *canonical doctrine*; distinguishes growth that merely departs from growth that returns to strengthen its source.
- `00_Foundation/16_Valere.md`: marked *canonical cornerstone*; valere as strength, wellness, worth; value directed toward what the receiver carries away.
- `00_Foundation/04_Grammar_of_the_Relay.md`: trace as what persists after an encounter.

**What the constitution gets right**
It recognizes memory, capability, relationships, and the future conditions of work as outcomes—not simply sales and completion. The phrase “what remains possible because the work was done well” embodies the source's return logic.

**Subtle source distinction**
Regeneration is **more than preservation or sustainability**: the source asks whether a result returns and enlarges the ground that made it possible. The constitution's “leave those conditions stronger” conveys this without jargon.

**No amendment proposed.**

### IX. Our Continuing Commitment

**Primary sources**
- `00_Foundation/02_Chain_of_Transmission.md`: invitation, not coercion; maker cannot guarantee transcendence.
- `00_Foundation/04_Grammar_of_the_Relay.md`: the trace re-enters the field; cycle continues.
- `00_Foundation/16_Valere.md`: “Vale — be well” as the orientation of value toward the receiver.
- `00_Foundation/28_Regeneration.md`: a return that enlarges the ground.

**What the constitution gets right**
The closing parallelism refuses overclaiming: we cannot compel meaning or own the culture, but we can tend the conditions. The final Basic City line makes the universal philosophy particular without reducing it to the brewery.

**No amendment proposed.**

## Three distinctions we must preserve

1. **Culture vs. mythology.** Culture is the repeated tending; mythology is how that tending is told and carried across time. The constitution does not need to become a mythology or a company origin story.
2. **Inner and outer culture.** Individual attention and capacity matter, but so do company-provided spaces, tools, rhythms, resources, and conditions. This prevents a cultural philosophy from becoming a demand that employees compensate for organizational shortcomings.
3. **Capacity vs. rank.** Formation can describe a relationship with work and responsibility without authorizing leadership to classify human worth or measure “stewardship” as spiritual status.

## What the vault deepens without demanding new wording

The vault adds concepts not explicitly named in Draft 0.1: Witness, three variables (Signal / Capacity / Transparency), identity as interference, the four modes of signal, culture/mythology as paired modes, *valere* as the outward orientation, and the regenerative return. These are **companion-level explanations** first. Their omission from a short, lived constitution is not automatically a defect.

## Editorial conclusion

**Draft 0.1 already carries the key form of the reformed framework:** cultivate the conditions; make well; receive rather than impose; pass the work onward; develop without ownership; tell the truth; return value to the ground. The dense vocabulary of the underlying source can remain available in this companion without entering the constitution.

**Recommendation at this stage: retain Draft 0.1 unchanged.** Future proposed amendments, if any, must be written outside the protected constitution and approved explicitly before editing it.
