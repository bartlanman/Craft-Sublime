# Mythogenesis Master Plate — Visual Brief
## The Birth of Culture Through Living Mythology

**Status:** Visual concept 0.1 / not canon / no amendment to Master Plate v4 or Cultural Constitution  
**Companion:** [Constitution of Mythology](MYTHOLOGY-CONSTITUTION.md)

## Governing visual question

**How can a lived encounter become a symbol and a story, and through repeated, free reception become a culture that changes the conditions of the next encounter?**

This plate is a visual inquiry, not a claim that cultural formation occurs in a deterministic sequence.

## Design lineage

- **Formal precedent:** the historical Master Plate V5 visual grammar (kraft stock, brass section medallions, industrial registration marks, axial gauge and instrument drawing, red measurement points, technical footer). **V5 was dropped July 2026**; it is a style and exploratory precedent, not source canon. Preserve Master Plate V4's status.
- **Cultural source:** [Mythology Constitution](MYTHOLOGY-CONSTITUTION.md) and the protected [Cultural Constitution](CULTURAL-CONSTITUTION.md).
- **Meaning Field:** relational semantics / living mythology work, especially traceable meaning, decompressibility, and source/interpretation difference.
- **TOS:** mythogenesis and symbolic transmission, plus the Shadow's warning about captured or imposed identities.
- **Symbolic Masterwork:** `THERE → HERE → FUNDAMENTAL → ENCOUNTER → GENESIS → CULTURAL LIFE`; an authored mark does not establish cultural adoption.
- **Basic City:** alchemical transformation as **analogy**; the spiral/needle and Plus Ultra beacon as particular marks; ordinary materials and encounters are the ground.

## Plate composition (proposed)

**Title:** THE BIRTH OF CULTURE  
**Subtitle:** A Living Mythology / Craft Sublime  
**Top axis:** MYTHOLOGY — PLUS ULTRA — CULTURE. These are related but not interchangeable.

Four large technical quadrants:

**I. THE SEARCH / THE UNKNOWN**  
*An invitation across the threshold.* Water / gold / elixir / home / meaning. A small ship between the Pillars of Hercules; the beacon illuminates possibility, not a promised destination. Main question: **WHAT CALLS US BEYOND THE KNOWN?**

**II. THE ORDEAL / THE ENCOUNTER**  
*Character becomes perceptible through relation and resistance.* Alchemical vessel / brewing vessel / shadow / cost / witness; maker, thing, receiver. Main question: **WHAT CHANGES IN THE SEARCHER?**

**III. THE TELLING / THE SYMBOL**  
*An encounter becomes tellable without being possessed.* Event, trace, memory, image, legend; symbol as compressed, re-openable meaning. Main question: **WHAT CAN CROSS INTO ANOTHER LIFE?**

**IV. THE CULTIVATION / THE RETURN**  
*Received stories become shared practices, and shared practices make new encounters possible.* Transmission, uptake, variation, challenge, ritual, regeneration. Main question: **WHAT IS RECEIVED, CHANGED, AND GIVEN AGAIN?**

A thin double-loop connective diagram across the quadrants:

`LIVED CONDITIONS → ENCOUNTER → WITNESSED TRACE → SYMBOL / TELLING → RECEPTION → REPEATED PRACTICE → CULTURE → NEW CONDITIONS`

The returning loop is **not** automatic. Depict an OPEN SEAM / small discontinuity at reception to represent freedom, refusal, and alternate interpretation. The loop must be drawn as a **spiral returning with difference**, not a closed corporate funnel.

**Bottom return:** `VALERE · WHAT WE LEAVE CAPABLE OF LIVING ON`

## Mythic counterpoints

- **Alchemy:** the object sought is gold / elixir / transformation; the deeper transformation may be in the searcher. Do not equate brewing with supernatural alchemy.
- **Odyssey / Jung:** journey, monsters, shadows, guides, return; analogy, not a mandatory psychological ladder.
- **Bacon / Plus Ultra:** the threshold of the known, the opening to inquiry; a complicated history, not science simply defeating alchemy.
- **Context:** facts, material conditions, inherited stories and social relations help determine what a symbol becomes.
- **Open mystery:** the plate makes room for the miraculous as subjective possibility, without asserting scientific proof.
- **Punctuated change:** environmental pressure can be represented as a possible interruption/acceleration of cultural habits, not as a scientific law of cultural engineering.

## Visual discipline

- Aged off-white/kraft parchment, monochrome engraved black technical illustrations; oxidized brass for medallions/rules; one restrained red signal/witness accent; a small green manual identifier; blue revision mark optional.
- Distinct geometric symbols and positive/negative space; no decorative borrowed sacred emblems, artificial ancient scripts, or archetype costumes.
- Use **real Basic City two marks** in final approved production, not AI-improvised substitutes. Early concepts may show simplified spiral/needle and pillars.
- Exact text must be typeset and proofread in a second vector pass; generative render may distort small lettering.
- Final plate is a **proposal** to be considered with user, never installed as canon by simply rendering it.

## What the plate must not do

- Must not imply mythology is imposed top-down or that community automatically accepts a narrative.
- Must not imply every individual must journey from Visitor to Steward as an employee rank.
- Must not erase factual/interpretive boundaries or claim quantum physics establishes unlimited possible futures.
- Must not show the myth as a tool for manipulating a clientele.
- Must not replace or edit the protected Cultural Constitution or the canonical Master Plate v4.
